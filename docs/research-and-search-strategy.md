# Research-led supplier acquisition

Research conducted 20 October 2026 by three GPT-6.1 Sol agents at high reasoning, covering the Indian market, supplier concerns and search architecture.

## Positioning
SR Infotech is a direct buyer of business inventory. The visitor is the stock owner or an authorised team member. Main message: “Your surplus. New possibilities. Let’s move it.” Primary CTA: “Sell your inventory.”

Confirmed by the owner: own logistics for pan-India pickup; willingness to consider untested electronics at a suitable price. Do not publish the commercial thresholds or terms.

## Evidence informing the content
- [BrandX](https://brandx.co.in/) provides a primary example of direct purchasing language and brand/channel concerns.
- [Excess2Sell overview](https://www.excess2sell.com/about) and [seller FAQ](https://www.excess2sell.com/cms/faq) illustrate the distinction between marketplace participation and a direct buyer.
- [Blubirch retailer case study](https://www.blubirch.in/case-study/multi-store-retailer-revenue-losses-from-returned-inventory) discusses the practical challenges of returned inventory and condition mismatches.
- [Prozo return processing](https://www.prozo.com/warehousing/return-processing-centres/) distinguishes potential routes after returned-stock assessment.
- [Uniserved for brands](https://liquidation.uniserved.com/for-brands) highlights manifests, condition and handover information.
- [B-Stock manifest guide](https://bstock.com/blog/buying-basics-all-about-manifests/) supports a structured stock list.
- [Liquidation.com seller requirements](https://www.liquidation.com/c/seller/index.html) supports accurate product and condition descriptions.

Competitor claims were used only to understand positioning and seller concerns. None were adopted as SR Infotech operating metrics or capabilities.

## SEO, AEO and AI discovery
The build uses static readable HTML, descriptive page titles, canonical URLs, local images, mobile layouts, related links, direct answers, visible FAQs, sitemap/RSS and meaningful structured data.

JSON-LD uses Organization, WebSite, Service, BreadcrumbList and BlogPosting where relevant. No fabricated reviews, ratings, Product offers or SoftwareApplication data.

[Google’s current AI optimization guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) says established SEO practices apply and no special AI schema is needed. We did not add an unsupported `llms.txt` ranking mechanism.

Visible FAQs serve readers. [Google’s documentation updates](https://developers.google.com/search/updates) record the retirement of FAQ rich results in 2026; the site does not sell FAQ schema as a guaranteed search feature.

Use [Google’s Organization guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/organization), [Article guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/article) and [breadcrumb guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/breadcrumb) when extending markup.

## Migration
[Google’s migration guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/site-move-with-url-changes) recommends relevant mappings and warns against unrelated redirects. Existing contact/blog/privacy navigation is retained; retired marketing and SaaS pages have no fabricated liquidation equivalents.
